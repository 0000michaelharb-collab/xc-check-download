FAQ

How often does a paraglider need an inspection?

The manufacturer sets the interval in the manual, usually 24 months or an hour limit.

How often do I need to repack my reserve?

As stated by the manufacturer, usually once a year.

When do I need to renew my tandem rating in Austria?

A check flight is required every 3 years, flown within the last 12 months before the deadline (§ 88 ZLPV 2006).

How do I install XC Check?

Open the link on your Android phone, tap the file and choose “Install”. If Android asks for permission for your browser, tap “Allow”.
